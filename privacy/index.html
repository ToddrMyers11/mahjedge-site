This policy covers both of our iPhone apps: Mahj Edge and Mahj Edge: Beginner Game Play.

Neither app collects, transmits or sells any personal information.

What the app stores

These are stored only on your iPhone, using Apple's on-device storage (SwiftData and UserDefaults).

Mahj Edge:

  • Exercise history: which exercises you answered, the result, the date and how long you took
  • Daily challenge completions and streaks
  • Settings: sound, haptics and reminder preferences

Mahj Edge: Beginner Game Play:

  • Lesson progress: which lessons you've finished, the lesson and step to resume, and how many times you've completed the guided practice

None of this leaves your device. There is no account, server, analytics, advertising or third-party SDK.

Notifications

Mahj Edge: Beginner Game Play doesn't send notifications. In Mahj Edge, daily reminders are off by default. If you turn them on, iOS asks for permission once, and the reminder is scheduled locally on your device. You can turn reminders off in the app or in the Settings app.

Purchases

Both apps are sold through the App Store. Apple processes payment, and we never receive or store payment information.

Deleting your data

In Mahj Edge, tap Settings → Reset All Progress. In Mahj Edge: Beginner Game Play, tap About → Reset Lesson Progress. Or delete the app. iCloud device backups may include the app's local data, under Apple's backup policies.

Children

Neither app knowingly collects information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
