Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026

SupportPrivacy Policy

Mahj Edge doesn't collect, transmit or sell any personal information.

What the app stores

These are stored only on your iPhone, using Apple's on-device storage (SwiftData and UserDefaults):

  • Exercise history: which exercises you answered, the result, the date and how long you took
  • Daily challenge completions and streaks
  • Settings: sound, haptics and reminder preferences

None of this leaves your device. There is no account, server, analytics, advertising or third-party SDK.

Notifications

Daily reminders are off by default. If you turn them on, iOS asks for permission once, and the reminder is scheduled locally on your device. You can turn reminders off in the app or in the Settings app.

Purchases

Mahj Edge is sold through the App Store. Apple processes payment, and Mahj Edge never receives or stores payment information.

Deleting your data

Tap Settings → Reset All Progress in the app, or delete the app. iCloud device backups may include the app's local data, under Apple's backup policies.

Children

Mahj Edge doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
