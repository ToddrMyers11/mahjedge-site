Frequently asked questions

Do the people I play with need the app?

No. You record every game on your iPhone. Other players are just names in your log.

What do I need to record for a game?

Only who played and who declared Mah Jongg, or that it was a wall game. Seats and scoring details (how it was won, who discarded, the hand value, jokerless and the card section) are optional.

How are payments worked out?

The app uses standard NMJL payments. On a discard, the discarder pays twice the hand value and everyone else pays the value. On a self-pick, everyone pays twice the value. A jokerless hand doubles the value, except Singles & Pairs. Wall games pay nothing. House rules aren't supported.

Can I see payments in money instead of points?

Yes. Go to About → Preferences → Show Payments In and choose Points, Cents or Dollars. One point counts as one cent.

Why don't some games count toward payment stats?

Payments need how the game was won, who discarded (for a discard win), the hand value and whether it was jokerless. Games missing any of these are left out of payment stats rather than counted as zero. You can edit a game to add them.

How do I fix a mistake?

Open the game from History and tap Edit Game, or swipe left on it. Your stats update right away.

Does it include the official National Mah Jongg League card?

No. The app isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the National Mah Jongg League.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. The app works completely offline and has no accounts, ads or in-app purchases.

Can I move my log to a new iPhone?

Your log is stored on your iPhone and is included in your regular iCloud or computer backup. Restoring that backup on a new iPhone brings your log with it.

How do I erase my log?

Tap About → Erase All Games and Players. This keeps your name and can't be undone.

How do I request a refund?

Apple handles all purchases. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request a refund.
