Frequently asked questions

What does the course cover?

Twelve short lessons take you from the object of the game through the tiles, building and breaking the wall, selecting tiles, organizing your hand, the Charleston, turn order, claiming discards, Jokers and winning. A guided practice and a glossary help you review.

Does it teach strategy?

No. This app teaches how the game is set up and played. Strategy, like choosing a hand or deciding what to pass, is covered in Mahj Edge, our strategy trainer.

Which rules does it follow?

Lessons follow the American Mah Jongg Association's published rules guide. House rules vary, so check yours with your table.

Does it include the official National Mah Jongg League card?

No. You'll need the current NMJL card to play. The app isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the National Mah Jongg League or the American Mah Jongg Association.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. The app works completely offline and has no accounts, ads or in-app purchases.

Can I pick up where I left off?

Yes. The app remembers the lesson and step you were on. Tap Resume on the Learn tab, or open any lesson to revisit it.

How do I start over?

Tap Restart course on the Learn tab, or About → Reset Lesson Progress. This can't be undone.

Can I move my progress to a new iPhone?

Progress is stored on your iPhone and is included in your regular iCloud or computer backup. Restoring that backup on a new iPhone brings your progress with it.

How do I request a refund?

Apple handles all purchases. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request a refund.
