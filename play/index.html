Frequently asked questions

What's free, and what does the full game add?

Your first 3 complete games are free. After that, one purchase unlocks the full game for good: unlimited games, by the house card or your own. There are no ads and no subscription, and the purchase works with Family Sharing.

What are the other purchases?

Three extra tables and four extra tile sets, including Large Print, sold one at a time or as a bundle. They change only how the game looks. The Green Baize table and Classic Ivory tiles are included.

How do I restore my purchases?

Tap Full Game, or choose a locked table or tile set, then tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I choose my opponents?

Tap Players. Give each opponent a name and a level: Easy, Medium or Strong. Changes apply straight away.

Does it include the official National Mah Jongg League card?

No. The house card is an original card made for this game. Mahj Edge Play isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the National Mah Jongg League.

Can I play by a card I own?

Yes. Tap Players → My card and enter its hands, or tap Scan hands from photos to read them from photos of your card. Then choose My own card. Your card stays on your device and is never shown during play: the game only uses it to check your Mahjong and to guide the opponents.

How do I get the best scan?

Photograph one panel of the card at a time so it fills the screen, with the card flat, from straight above, in good even light. Keep color on, because the colors tell the suits apart. Check every hand against the line from your photo shown above it, and tap a hand to correct it before adding it.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Photos are read on your device and aren't kept after the hands are added.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. The game works completely offline. Buying and restoring purchases needs a connection to the App Store.

How do I reset the score?

Tap Players, then Reset under Score.

How do I request a refund?

Apple handles all purchases. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request a refund.
